Privacy Policy — FitPilot

Last updated 13 September 2026

FitPilot is a fitness and nutrition app operated by Wameed Tech. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to get it deleted.

Two commitments up front: we do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising. Health and fitness data read from Health Connect is never shared with any third party, including our AI provider.

Who we are

Wameed Tech is the data controller for FitPilot. You can reach us about anything in this policy at info@wameedtech.com.

What we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and a password, or — if you choose Google sign-in — your email address and basic profile information supplied by Google. We never receive your Google password. One email address may use one sign-in method; we do not link accounts.

Health and fitness data

If, and only if, you tap Connect to health app and grant permission, FitPilot reads the following from Google Health Connect:

  • Steps
  • Sleep duration
  • Resting heart rate
  • Active calories burned
  • Weight

Read access only — FitPilot never writes to Health Connect and never modifies data held by other apps. The connection is off until you turn it on, and you can revoke it at any time in the Health Connect settings on your device.

We use this data for one purpose: to make your plan match your actual activity. Measured step counts adjust your daily calorie target and training volume, because a guess made once during setup is less accurate than what your phone measures every day.

Body composition scans

If you upload an InBody or similar body composition report, we store the figures extracted from it (body fat percentage, skeletal muscle mass, visceral fat) and use them to adjust your plan. The uploaded image itself is deleted automatically within 24 hours.

Progress and usage

We store the workouts you mark complete, weight and waist check-ins you record, meals you log, and messages you send to the AI coach. We also store a push notification token so we can send your weekly plan.

What we do not collect

  • Payment card details — any subscription is processed by Google Play, which does not share your card with us
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertisers. We may disclose data if legally required to do so.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in the Amazon Web Services Frankfurt region in the European Union. Requests to OpenAI may be processed in the United States under standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Uploaded body composition images are deleted within 24 hours of upload. When you delete your account, your profile, plans, progress, health data, and coach conversations are deleted within 30 days.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and everything stored with it from inside the app: Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. It is immediate and cannot be undone.

You can also ask us to do it by emailing info@wameedtech.com from the address you registered with. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Disconnecting Health Connect stops any further reading of your health data immediately. To also remove the health data we have already stored, use the deletion request above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, receive a copy in a portable format, object to processing, or withdraw consent. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened. To exercise any of these, email us at the address above. If you are in the EU or UK and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

FitPilot is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Access to production systems is restricted, and sign-in credentials are handled by Amazon Cognito rather than stored by us. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat health data as sensitive and limit who and what can reach it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

بيانات الصحة واللياقة

إذا قمت — وفقط إذا قمت — بالضغط على الاتصال بتطبيق الصحة ومنحت الإذن، يقرأ فيت بايلوت ما يلي من Google Health Connect:

  • عدد الخطوات
  • مدة النوم
  • معدل ضربات القلب أثناء الراحة
  • السعرات الحرارية النشطة المحروقة
  • الوزن

صلاحية القراءة فقط — لا يكتب فيت بايلوت أي بيانات في Health Connect ولا يعدّل البيانات التي تحتفظ بها التطبيقات الأخرى. يبقى الاتصال متوقفاً حتى تقوم أنت بتفعيله، ويمكنك إلغاؤه في أي وقت من إعدادات Health Connect على جهازك.

نستخدم هذه البيانات لغرض واحد: جعل خطتك مطابقة لنشاطك الفعلي. فعدد الخطوات المقاس يعدّل هدفك اليومي من السعرات وحجم التمرين، لأن تقديراً يُعطى مرة واحدة عند الإعداد أقل دقة مما يقيسه هاتفك كل يوم.

تحاليل تركيب الجسم

إذا رفعت تقرير InBody أو تقريراً مشابهاً لتركيب الجسم، فإننا نخزّن الأرقام المستخرجة منه (نسبة الدهون، كتلة العضلات الهيكلية، الدهون الحشوية) ونستخدمها لتعديل خطتك. أما الصورة المرفوعة نفسها فتُحذف تلقائياً خلال 24 ساعة.

التقدّم والاستخدام

نخزّن التمارين التي تسجّلها كمكتملة، وقياسات الوزن والخصر التي تدوّنها، والوجبات التي تسجّلها، والرسائل التي ترسلها إلى المدرب الذكي. كما نخزّن رمز الإشعارات لنتمكّن من إرسال خطتك الأسبوعية.

حذف حسابك وبياناتك

إن قطع الاتصال بـ Health Connect يوقف فوراً أي قراءة إضافية لبيانات صحتك. ولحذف بيانات الصحة التي خزّناها مسبقاً، استخدم طلب الحذف أعلاه.
