Privacy Policy — Noor ALDEEN

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Noor ALDEEN (“the app”) is published by Wameed Tech. This policy explains exactly what the app does with data. We have tried to describe this precisely rather than generally.

The app has no user accounts. You never create a login, and we never ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

1. Location

The app requests your approximate location to calculate prayer times and the direction of the Qibla. Both calculations run entirely on your device using an offline astronomical algorithm.

Your coordinates are stored only on your phone and are never sent to Wameed Tech. We operate no server and hold no database of users.

One exception, stated for accuracy: to display your city name in the app, your coordinates are passed to your device operating system’s built-in geocoding service (provided by Google on Android and Apple on iOS). This is handled by the operating system, not by us.

You may refuse or revoke the location permission at any time in your device settings. The app will continue to work; prayer times and Qibla will not.

2. Advertising

The free version displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob. To do this, AdMob accesses your device’s advertising identifier and may collect device and usage information to select and measure ads, including personalised ads where you have consented.

This data is collected by Google, not by us. It is governed by Google’s Privacy & Terms.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings. Premium subscribers see no advertising at all.

3. Notifications

Prayer alerts

Athan notifications are scheduled on your device and require no internet connection. No data leaves your phone for these.

Announcements

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) to send occasional announcements, such as Ramadan greetings. Firebase assigns your installation an anonymous device token so a message can reach it. This token identifies an app installation, not you personally.

You can turn announcements off in Settings, which unsubscribes your device.

4. Quran text and recitation

Quran text is retrieved from api.alquran.cloud and recitation audio is streamed from cdn.islamic.network. When the app requests these, those services receive your IP address, as any website would. We do not control these services and receive no data from them.

Downloaded recitations are stored on your device only.

5. Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play and managed through RevenueCat. Payment happens entirely inside Google Play. We never see or store your card details.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase receipt in order to confirm your subscription is active. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

6. Data stored on your device

The following stay on your phone and are removed when you uninstall the app: your settings, last known coordinates and city name, Tasbeeh counts, cached Quran text, and any recitations you downloaded.

8. Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete. To remove everything the app stores, uninstall it. To limit advertising data, reset your advertising identifier in your device settings.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page is updated. Material changes will be announced in the app.
